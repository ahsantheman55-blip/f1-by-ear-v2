How to play

VoiceOver

VoiceOver takes over the screen, so the pedals cannot work while it is on. Choose Automatic speed control in Settings and you only steer, so VoiceOver can stay on: double-tap Start lights. For pedals, double-tap Race, then triple-click the side button to turn VoiceOver off, and triple-click again after the race.

Holding the phone

Hold the phone sideways in both hands like a steering wheel, screen facing you, and turn it left and right. Either landscape direction works. Wear headphones so you hear left and right.

Touches

  • Accelerate: hold one thumb on the screen. Coast: lift off. Brake: hold two thumbs.
  • Gear, speed and position: tap twice quickly with two fingers, or double-tap the Info button.
  • Pause: tap with three fingers. Tap with three fingers again to carry on. Hold three fingers for two seconds while paused to leave.

Your race engineer

  • Corner calls come early, for example "Right hairpin in 250, heavy braking", then "Brake now", then "Turn right" as you reach it. A chicane is "Chicane, left then right", and you hear "Now left" for the second part.
  • Words for corners, slowest to fastest: hairpin, tight, slow, medium, fast. "Flat out" means no braking. "Long" means hold the wheel for a while. "Tightens" means the second part is slower.
  • Where you are: "Wide right, steer left" when you drift to the edge, and "Off track, steer left" if you leave the road.
  • Commentary in Settings: corners only, corners and positions, or full commentary with overtakes, gaps, rival mistakes, slipstream and gears.

Sounds

  • Engine: eight gears. Revs climb in each gear, then drop with a crackle at each upshift. Under braking you hear blips and pops as it changes down. Hold it flat in top gear in a slipstream and it bounces off the limiter.
  • Your engine sits where your car is across the track: in your left ear means you are on the left.
  • Tyres: a hiss means you are near the limit of grip, a squeal means you are over it.
  • Kerbs rumble at the edge, gravel crunches and slows you, barriers crash. A big hit at speed is a crash: the car stops for three seconds, then you go again.
  • Rivals are heard where they are. Pull out of a car's lane to pass. Close behind a car on a straight you get a slipstream, with a rushing wind.
  • The start: five beeps and numbers for the five red lights, a pause, then "Lights out, go".
